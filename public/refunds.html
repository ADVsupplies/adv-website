Refunds & Returns

What to do if something is wrong with your order.

If there is a problem with your order

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have goods repaired or replaced if they fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

If you received the wrong product, the wrong quantity or faulty goods, call us within [48 hours] of delivery or pick up with your order number and, if you can, a photo. We will put it right with a replacement, a refund or a credit. We may need to inspect the product first.

Change of mind: bulk materials

We do not offer refunds if you simply change your mind about bulk materials once they have been loaded or tipped. Loose material cannot be collected and resold. Please check your quantities before ordering, or call us and we will help you work out what you need.

Change of mind: bagged products, hardware and accessories

  • Return unused items in their original condition and packaging within 7 days with your receipt.
  • We will give you a [exchange / store credit].
  • Not accepted for change of mind: opened bags, cut-to-length items, mixed or tinted products, chemicals that have been opened, and special orders.
