Privacy Policy

How ADV Supplies collects, uses and protects your personal information.

This policy explains how ADV Garden and Building Supplies Pty Ltd (ABN [ABN]) ("ADV Supplies", "we", "us") handles personal information. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your delivery address, delivery date and site notes (such as where to tip and gate codes)
  • Your order history and what you bought
  • Payment details, which are entered on Square's secure checkout. We never see or store your full card number.
  • If you sign up to our emails: your email address and which emails you open

Why we collect it

  • To take your order, deliver it and send you a receipt
  • To contact you about your delivery, including on the day
  • To answer questions and handle returns or complaints
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as tax records
  • To send you offers and news, only if you have agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share what each service needs to do its job:

  • Square, which processes payments and keeps our sales records
  • Detrack, which our drivers use to see and complete deliveries
  • Google, which helps you search for and confirm your address
  • Make, which passes order details between these systems
  • HubSpot, which sends our emails if you have signed up
  • Our accountants and IT providers, and government agencies when the law requires it

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose providers that protect data to a standard similar to the Australian Privacy Principles. We never sell your personal information.

Keeping it safe

We use secure, password-protected systems and limit access to staff who need it. We keep records only as long as we need them for the reasons above or as the law requires.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below and we will reply within 30 days.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, tell us first and we will try to fix it. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
