Terms & Conditions of Sale

The terms that apply when you order from ADV Supplies online, by phone or at the yard.

1. Orders and prices

  • All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.
  • Bulk materials are priced per cubic metre (m³) and sold in quarter-metre steps. For 10 m³ or more, call us for bulk pricing.
  • Your order is confirmed once payment goes through. We will contact you if an item is unavailable and offer a swap or a refund for that item.
  • We may correct obvious pricing errors before your order is delivered or collected.

2. Delivery

  • Delivery is charged at a flat rate per truck load, based on your suburb's delivery zone. One truck carries up to 10 m³ of one material, or two materials in separate compartments.
  • The minimum bulk delivery is 1 m³. For less, you can pick up from the yard.
  • We deliver Monday to Saturday. We will do our best to deliver on the day you choose, but times can change due to weather, traffic or breakdowns. We will let you know if your delivery is delayed.
  • Our trucks are insured to stay on the road. We tip on your driveway, crossover or nature strip only. We cannot drive onto lawns, into backyards or onto soft ground.
  • You are responsible for clear, safe access for a heavy truck: no low wires, overhanging branches, parked cars or narrow gates in the way, and enough room for the tray to lift.
  • When you order, you choose where we tip: your driveway, crossover or nature strip. You are responsible for choosing a spot that is safe and suitable for the load.
  • If you ask our driver to tip somewhere else, or somewhere that turns out to be unsafe or unsuitable, you accept responsibility for any damage, injury or extra cost that results. Our driver may still decline to tip where they believe it is unsafe.
  • A loaded truck is heavy. We take care, but we are not responsible for damage to driveways, kerbs, paving or underground pipes on the spot you choose for us to tip, unless we caused it through our own negligence.
  • Material is your responsibility once it has been tipped. Please cover it if rain or wind is likely, and keep children away from the pile.

3. Pick up from the yard

  • Collect from 320 Hallam Rd, Hampton Park during opening hours (8am to 4pm).
  • We load your vehicle or trailer, but you are responsible for not overloading it and for securing the load before you drive away.

4. Natural products

Mulch, soil, sand, pebbles, rock and firewood are natural products. Colour, size, texture and moisture vary from load to load and may differ from photos. Our material calculator gives an estimate only. Allow a little extra for compaction and settling.

5. Changes and cancellations

  • To change or cancel a delivery, call us before [12pm the day before] delivery.
  • Once a load has been loaded or has left the yard, we cannot cancel it. See our Refunds & Returns policy for your options.

6. TRADE ACCOUNTS

Trade account customers are also bound by their trade account agreement. If it conflicts with these terms, the trade account agreement applies.
