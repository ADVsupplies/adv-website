Gift Card Terms

Terms for ADV Supplies gift cards.

  • Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase. The expiry date is shown on the card or in the email.
  • Use your gift card online or at the yard at 320 Hallam Rd, Hampton Park, for any product or delivery.
  • Your gift card can be used across more than one purchase until the balance runs out. There is no minimum spend.
  • No fees are charged after purchase. Check your balance any time by calling us or asking at the counter.
  • Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires.
  • Treat your gift card like cash. If it is lost or stolen, we cannot replace it unless you have proof of purchase and the balance has not been used.
  • If you return goods bought with a gift card, the refund goes back onto a gift card.
  • Promotional or free vouchers may have different terms, which will be shown on the voucher.
