About ADV

Our story

Formerly
Diaco's Discount Nursery

ADV Supplies was formerly Diaco's Discount Nursery. For 22 years, Diaco's supplied gardens across Melbourne's south-east from our yard on Hallam Road.

On 1 June 2026 we became ADV Garden and Building Supplies. Same yard at 320 Hallam Rd, Hampton Park, with a new name and a full focus on bulk landscape and building supplies for homes, builders and landscapers.

What we do

Bulk landscape suppliesSoil, compost, mulch, sand, pebbles, rocks, toppings and screenings, sold by the cubic metre.
